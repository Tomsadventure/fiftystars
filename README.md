# Fifty Stars

Learn the 50 U.S. states – their flags, their capitals, their three largest cities and where they are on the map. Every state you
really know gets a stamp in your passport and lights one of the fifty stars. Spaced repetition brings back what you are about to
forget. For iPad, iPhone and computer; works offline (Safari → Share → «Add to Home Screen»).

This folder is generated by `Scripts/bauen.py` in the project `FiftyStars` – do not edit it by hand, it gets overwritten.

Sources: map, city populations and locations © U.S. Census Bureau (Cartographic Boundary Files 2024, Vintage 2025 population
estimates, 2025 Gazetteer; Hawaii: 2020 Census) – public domain. Flags: Flagpedia / flagcdn.com and Wikimedia Commons (public domain).
Postcards and pictures: drawn with OpenAI. Voice: ElevenLabs. Fonts «Alfa Slab One» and «Libre Franklin» under the SIL Open Font
License (`fonts/OFL-AlfaSlabOne.txt`, `fonts/OFL-LibreFranklin.txt`).
